# Writing job specs

A job spec says **what and why, never how**. The teammate is a specialist; it decides the steps.

- **teammate**: the one whose role fits (`crew_status` if unsure). One writer per target: don't hand the same file,
  ticket or record to two teammates.
- **objective**: what done looks like and why it matters. "Find why Mobile Safari signups dropped since Nov 2, so
  the owner can decide whether to roll back the pricing page."
- **context**: links, ids, numbers and decisions already made. Never paste secrets.
- **constraints**: limits that matter (budget, deadline, "don't contact customers", "read-only").
- **output_shape**: what to hand back ("a 3-line summary and a table of the top 5").

Don't split the work into steps for it, and don't name tools. The person who asked pays for the job, and sees the
hand-off in the thread.
