# Peeblo artwork

Both PNGs were generated using the built-in image generation tool for this project. Final source images are preserved unmodified here. Video keyframes reuse these files.

## peeblo-ledger-orbit.png
Prompt: Create a refined editorial 3D still life for Peeblo, an autonomous accounts receivable teammate website. Wide landscape 1536x1024. A single sculptural loop of folded pale ivory paper, like a long ledger ribbon resolving into a smooth continuous orbit, balanced on a small dark green stone pedestal. A few fine emerald translucent glass tabs embedded along the paper edge suggest reconciled records. Physical paper grain, precise folds, brushed stone, subtle green reflected light. Deep warm charcoal studio background, directional soft light from upper left, grounded shadow. Subject occupies right 65 percent with clean dark negative space on left. Museum-quality product photography, quiet, tactile, financially literate, sophisticated. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no charts, no UI, no coins, no robot, no glowing neon. This is a website illustration asset, not a screenshot.

## peeblo-evidence.png
Prompt: Wide editorial still life photograph for Peeblo an autonomous finance teammate. A stack of ivory ledger papers neatly aligned with a sculptural forest-green translucent glass paperweight in the shape of two interlocking smooth arches. Small brushed aluminum indexing tabs, one paper corner gently lifted. Tactile grain and calm side lighting. Warm charcoal background and surface, almost monochrome with restrained forest green, isolated composition centered, wide 1536x1024. Museum product photography, close detailed materials, quiet confident financial editorial identity. No writing no text no letters no numbers no logos no UI no robots no coins no glow. Companion asset to an ivory folded paper ribbon on green stone.
