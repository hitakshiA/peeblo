## Design Context

### Users and purpose
Peeblo is an autonomous accounts receivable teammate for B2B finance and revenue operations teams. It owns exceptions, reconciles existing payments, follows up on commitments, and works across Stripe, QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, Jira, and Dropbox. The hackathon demo should show independent background work with a clear business outcome, not a chat interface or a fixed workflow builder.

### Brand personality
Calm, exact, accountable. A working teammate that brings back a result and its evidence. Preserve the user's requested Manthan layout language and font styling while introducing original Peeblo imagery and a more animated presentation.

### Aesthetic direction
Dark editorial finance desk. Retain Geist Sans, Geist Mono for compact record labels, and Instrument Serif italic emphasis. Preserve the centered hero, large visual showcase below, narrative band, immersive apps panel, split feature sections, closing scene, and large footer wordmark. Original ivory-paper and green-glass still-life assets connect financial records to calm follow-through. The supplied exact ThreeUI emerald pixel horizon and living green scene add controlled motion.

### Design principles
1. State a recognizable business problem and show a resolved case.
2. Motion explains progress or adds restrained material movement; keep text stable and readable.
3. All interactions work with keyboard, mobile, and reduced motion; respect the operating system preference without a visible motion toggle.
4. Keep demo data and planned capabilities distinguishable from functioning integrations.
5. Financial action requires policy authority, result verification, and duplicate prevention.
6. Preserve supplied shader source; integrate through host wrappers without modifying its authored internals.

### Copy direction
Use direct role, task, and outcome language, informed by the user-provided Userlens and Rex examples. Remove decorative all-caps section labels, abstract contrast slogans, hackathon/prototype footer copy, and visible pause controls. The hero media spans the entire opening, including navigation and headline.

### Product animation
Use original Peeblo case scenes inspired by the motion techniques in the user-supplied Rex screenshots: drawn card outlines and connector paths, typed field contents, and a cursor with eased approach, correction, and click feedback. Keep Peeblo's dark palette and its own Northstar reconciliation case. Loop the story with a readable result hold. Do not reintroduce the static dashboard or visible pause controls.

### Latest layout preferences
A simple single-counter P mark replaces the tangled line mark. Integration logos scroll continuously, rotate their selected state, and never show a scrollbar. The paper-trail/reliability section is removed. Give the hero a taller blurred fade into the next section.
